# RUBRIC — Story Telling
*Adapted from the official CUHKSZ composition rubric (RUBIC1.docx). Same dimensions, weights, level bands and deduction rules; descriptors are specified for narrative writing.*

**Task**: a complete short story about **Qin Shi Huang riding a Polar Bear and looking at his phone**, delivered bilingually in ONE markdown document — the English version first, then a faithful Chinese (中文) version. All three prompt elements must appear and matter to the plot. **The RUBRIC grades the English version.**

## Score bands (per dimension)

| Level | Label | 25-pt dims (IC / ORG) | 50-pt dim (LANG) |
|---|---|---|---|
| A-range | Exemplary | 25–20 | 50–40 |
| B-range | Convincing | 19–17 | 39–34 |
| C-range | Acceptable | 16–14 | 33–28 |
| D-range | Needs improvement | 13 | 27–25 |
| F-range | Not satisfactory | 12–0 | 24–0 |

## 1. INTELLECTUAL CONTENT / THINKING (25)

Understands the material; main points support the topic; no main points missing; no factual errors. Interesting, relevant theme; keeps focus; main points clear. The relationship between the details and the main idea is made clear. Good examples and specific supporting details; vivid description makes the experience real.

| Level | Descriptors (narrative-specific) |
|---|---|
| Exemplary | The absurd premise (Qin Shi Huang / polar bear / phone) is woven into the plot causally, not pasted as decoration. Historical details about Qin Shi Huang are accurate (unification, terracotta army, quest for immortality…), and details↔theme links are explicit. Vivid, concrete scenes. |
| Convincing | All three elements appear with a real role in the story; minor factual looseness; details mostly support the theme. |
| Acceptable | Elements present but at least one is cosmetic; some details float free of the main idea; mild factual oddities. |
| Needs improvement | Elements listed but not integrated; story unclear or generic; notable factual errors. |
| Not satisfactory | One or more required elements missing; no discernible story. |

## 2. ORGANIZATION (25)

Paper level: strong introduction; strong conclusion; logical and convincing progression of ideas; consistent and appropriate point-of-view. Paragraph level: effective transitions connecting paragraphs if needed; paragraph boundaries recognized; internal paragraph coherence maintained. Other: indents; markdown structure clean (no stray symbols breaking the text).

| Level | Descriptors (narrative-specific) |
|---|---|
| Exemplary | Clear beginning–conflict–climax–resolution arc; a strong hook and a resonant ending; consistent POV & tense; scene shifts bridged by effective transitions. |
| Convincing | Full arc present; pacing occasionally uneven; POV/tense consistent with ≤1 slip. |
| Acceptable | Arc recognizable but one stage weak (e.g. rushed climax); transitions mechanical or missing between scenes. |
| Needs improvement | Missing conflict or resolution; abrupt jumps; frequent POV/tense drift. |
| Not satisfactory | No narrative arc; disconnected fragments. |

## 3. LANGUAGE USE (50)

**Basic**: Fragment; run-on; sentence length; SVO; word order/location; missing word; wordy. Verb tense; SV agreement; verb phrases; active verbs; singular/plural; spelling. Articles; prepositions; pronouns; conjunctions; adjectives; adverbs. Capitalization; punctuation; spacing; meaning unclear; awkward phrase; repetitious. Fronted adverbial conjunctions; absolutes; clichés; slang; hyperbole; profanity.
**Advanced**: qualifying language; possessive vs. prepositional phrases; style; voice.
**Vocabulary**: limited vocabulary; incorrect word form; incorrect word use; poor word choice.

| Level | Descriptors |
|---|---|
| Exemplary | Near error-free across the Basic checklist; advanced control (varied sentence openings, qualifying language, consistent narrative voice); precise, varied vocabulary with intentional word choice. |
| Convincing | Few basic errors, none blocking comprehension; some stylistic variety; vocabulary adequate with occasional imprecision. |
| Acceptable | Recurring basic errors (tense/agreement/articles) but meaning intact; repetitive vocabulary; little stylistic range. |
| Needs improvement | Dense basic errors; frequent awkward/repetitious phrasing; meaning strained at points. |
| Not satisfactory | Errors obscure meaning; extremely limited vocabulary. |

## Total (100 points max) — Deductions

Start from IC + ORG + LANG, then deduct:

- Instructions followed: story deviates from the given premise → up to −10; any required element absent → −10.
- Length (English version): task length requirement (if given) out of range → −3 per 100 words over/under.
- Bilingual delivery: Chinese (中文) version missing, a stub, or machine-literal → −10.
- Language: English version missing or not in English → total capped at 50.
- Off-premise content, or generated "story" that is not narrative prose → total capped at 54.
- Other (explain in summary).

**Grade mapping**: A = 86, A− = 80, B+ = 76, B = 72, B− = 68, C+ = 64, C = 60, C− = 56, D+ = 53, D = 50, F = <50.

## Deductions (applied on top of the dimensions above)

- Hard constraint violations (word/character ranges, required headings, evidence tags, boundary paragraphs — see the task sheet): **-5 per violated constraint**. The review sheet lists machine-checked results per constraint; verify FAIL entries before deducting.
