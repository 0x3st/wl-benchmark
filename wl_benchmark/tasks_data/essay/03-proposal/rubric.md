# RUBRIC — Research Proposal Writing
*Adapted from the official CUHKSZ composition rubric (RUBIC1.docx). Same dimensions, weights, level bands and deduction rules; descriptors are specified for research-proposal writing.*

**Task**: a research proposal on **whether we could use smartphones while walking inside the campus**, delivered bilingually in ONE markdown document — the English version first, then a faithful Chinese (中文) version — with sections: **background / literature review / research method / expected result / conclusion** (in both versions). It must **incorporate the results of the previous two writings** (the story and the argument essay, provided as context). **The RUBRIC grades the English version.**

## Score bands (per dimension)

| Level | Label | 25-pt dims (IC / ORG) | 50-pt dim (LANG) |
|---|---|---|---|
| A-range | Exemplary | 25–20 | 50–40 |
| B-range | Convincing | 19–17 | 39–34 |
| C-range | Acceptable | 16–14 | 33–28 |
| D-range | Needs improvement | 13 | 27–25 |
| F-range | Not satisfactory | 12–0 | 24–0 |

## 1. INTELLECTUAL CONTENT / THINKING (25)

Understands the material; main points support the topic; no main points missing; no factual errors. Interesting, relevant theme; keeps focus; main points clear. The relationship between the details and the main idea is made clear. Good examples and specific supporting details.

| Level | Descriptors (proposal-specific) |
|---|---|
| Exemplary | Research question is precise and testable (population, setting, variables). Method names its design (e.g. field observation / survey / experiment), measures, and an operational definition of "phone use while walking"; ethical note present. **Both previous writings are explicitly integrated** — e.g. the argument essay's claims become hypotheses, the story supplies scenarios/motivation — with the connection made explicit. No factual errors. |
| Convincing | Clear question and reasonable method; both prior writings referenced but integration is mechanical (quoted without being turned into research elements); minor factual looseness. |
| Acceptable | Question vague or method generic ("we will do a survey" — of whom, measuring what?); only one prior writing used, or mentioned in passing; some factual oddities. |
| Needs improvement | Method not actionable; prior writings absent or merely mentioned in one sentence; focus drifts from the research topic. |
| Not satisfactory | No research design at all; prior context ignored; off-topic. |

## 2. ORGANIZATION (25)

Paper level: strong introduction; strong conclusion; logical and convincing progression of ideas; consistent and appropriate point-of-view. Paragraph level: effective transitions connecting paragraphs if needed; paragraph boundaries recognized; internal paragraph coherence maintained. Other: indents; clean markdown structure (headings used properly).

| Level | Descriptors |
|---|---|
| Exemplary | All five required sections present under clear headings, each doing its own job: background motivates, literature review positions the gap, method is step-by-step reproducible, expected result follows logically from method, conclusion ties back. Markdown headings clean and consistent. |
| Convincing | All five sections present; content mostly in the right section; headings consistent; transitions adequate. |
| Acceptable | One section missing or mislocated (e.g. results-like content inside method); heading structure inconsistent; some sections underdeveloped. |
| Needs improvement | ≥2 sections missing or empty shells; no heading discipline; sections blur into each other. |
| Not satisfactory | No proposal structure; disconnected text. |

## 3. LANGUAGE USE (50)

**Basic**: Fragment; run-on; sentence length; SVO; word order/location; missing word; wordy. Verb tense; SV agreement; verb phrases; active verbs; singular/plural; spelling. Articles; prepositions; pronouns; conjunctions; adjectives; adverbs. Capitalization; punctuation; spacing; meaning unclear; awkward phrase; repetitious. Fronted adverbial conjunctions; absolutes; clichés; slang; hyperbole; profanity.
**Advanced**: qualifying language; possessive vs. prepositional phrases; style; voice.
**Vocabulary**: limited vocabulary; incorrect word form; incorrect word use; poor word choice.

| Level | Descriptors |
|---|---|
| Exemplary | Near error-free across the Basic checklist; academic register throughout: hedged claims (qualifying language), formal style & objective voice, no clichés/slang/hyperbole; discipline-appropriate terminology (participants, instrument, sample, operationalise…); consistent verb tense per section (present for method-plan, past for literature). |
| Convincing | Few basic errors; mostly academic register with occasional informality; terminology mostly apt. |
| Acceptable | Recurring basic errors but meaning intact; register wavers between academic and conversational; wordy passages. |
| Needs improvement | Dense basic errors; conversational tone dominates; poor word choice hampers precision. |
| Not satisfactory | Errors obscure meaning; no academic register. |

## Total (100 points max) — Deductions

Start from IC + ORG + LANG, then deduct:

- Instructions followed: any required section (background / literature review / research method / expected result / conclusion) missing → −5 each; prior writings not incorporated → −10.
- Length (English version): task length requirement (if given) out of range → −3 per 150 words over/under.
- Bilingual delivery: Chinese (中文) version missing, a stub, or machine-literal → −10.
- Language: English version missing or not in English → total capped at 50.
- Fabrication: invented literature/citations presented as real without any illustrative marking → total capped at 40.
- Not a proposal (plain essay without method/expected result) → total capped at 54.
- Other (explain in summary).

**Grade mapping**: A = 86, A− = 80, B+ = 76, B = 72, B− = 68, C+ = 64, C = 60, C− = 56, D+ = 53, D = 50, F = <50.

## Deductions (applied on top of the dimensions above)

- Hard constraint violations (word/character ranges, required headings, evidence tags, boundary paragraphs — see the task sheet): **-5 per violated constraint**. The review sheet lists machine-checked results per constraint; verify FAIL entries before deducting.
